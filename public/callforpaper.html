Call For Paper

THE 18TH IMT-GT INTERNATIONAL CONFFERENCES ON MATHEMATICS, STATISTICS AND THEIR APPLICATIONS (ICMSA) 2023

Banda Aceh, Indonesia, April 10-11, 2023

Full Paper Submission Deadline: March 07, 2023
Camera Ready: April 07, 2023

It is our great pleasure to welcome you to the 18th IMT_GT International Conference on Mathematics, Statistics and Their Applications (ICMSA) 2023. The conference will be held on April 10-11, 2023, in Banda Aceh, Indonesia. The 18th IMT-GT ICMSA 2022 is hosted and organized by Department of Statistics, Universitas Syiah Kuala (USK). This conference is also co-organized by the Forum Pendidikan Tingi Statistika (FORSTAT).

Under the theme "Advancing Data Science in Accelerating Achievement of SDGs", the conference will provide an excellent forum for knowledge sharing and research findings related to data science and artificial intelligence research and innovations for the latest technology development to enrich intelligent societies.
