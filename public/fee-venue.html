Notes :

  • FORSTAT Member will get 20% discount.
  • One standard registration covers the registration of one paper and the participation of one presenter.
  • Every participant must register separately: if two co-authors attend, each of them has to register.
  • An author with the additional paper: If an author (the first author) has two (or more) accepted papers (as the first author), per additional papers will be charged US$ 150 (international participants) or IDR 1,500,000 (domestic participants).
  • An author with the additional pages: per additional page will be charged US$ 50 (international participants) or IDR 500,000 (domestic participants).
  • Participants are eligible for student rate if he/she is a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate student. The student must be the first author. After paper acceptance notification from conference committee, the students must email the scanned copy of their ‘Student Identification Card’ to icmsa@usk.ac.id within paper ID.
  • Registration fee includes the two days of the conference (all sessions and keynotes), book of abstracts, and all sessions of KBI for all participants.
  • Certificates only given to the participant or attendee have registered their attendance at the conference registration.
