Keynote Speakers

Prof. Dr. Sebastian Vollmer
University of Göttingen, Germany
"Coming Soon"

Sebastian Vollmer is Professor of Development Economics and currently serves as Director of the Centre for Modern Indian Studies at the University of Göttingen. His research examines the linkages between poverty and health in low- and middle-income countries. Sebastian Vollmer studied Mathematics and Economics at the University of Göttingen and also received his PhD in Economics from the University of Göttingen. He was Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Harvard University, Visiting Assistant Professor of Economics at Dartmouth College and Adjunct Professor of Global Health at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. He has worked as a consultant for the World Bank, UNICEF, UNDP, UNESCO, FAO and several other organizations.

Prof. Dr.rer.pol Heri Kuswanto, M.Si
Institute Teknologi Sepuluh November, Indonesia
"Leveraging Satellite Imagery for Statistical Prediction : Case Studies on Official Statistics"

Upon completing his doctoral degree at Leibniz Hannover University, Germany, Mr. Heri Kuswanto did his post-doctoral on Calibration of ensemble weather forecast at Laval University, Canada. Since then, most of his works are dealing with extreme climate modeling and prediction. Mr. Kuswanto also does many activities i.e. Secretary General of Indonesia Young Science Academy, Research affiliate at Harvard University and get many awards such as; Harvard Residency Program, Research Ambassador of DAAD Jakarta, Elected member of Indonesian Young Science Academy, and Listed as 15 Best Lecturers (Academic Staff) in Indonesia by Ministry of Higher Education.

Prof. Dr. Don McNeil
Prince of Songkla University, Thailand
"Using Spline Functions to Analyse Survival Data"

After spending six years as Associate Professor in the Statistics Department at Princeton University, I was appointed as foundation Professor of Statistics in the School of Economic & Financial Studies at Macquarie University in 1976, and was invited to address its graduates in 1998 before retiring in 2001 and helping to run a graduate program at Prince of Songkla University in Thailand (see my piece in the book "Who Says I Am Retired" published in 2018 by the Social Security Research Centre at the University of Malaya, where one of my former PhD students Halimah Awang works).
